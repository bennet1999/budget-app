What happens when

Open calendar →

    Income & starting point

    Your take-home pay after tax and deductions.

    Appraisals & raises

    Salary changes from the month you choose. Percentages stack on top of each other.

    Extra income

    Bonuses and other lump sums you expect — once or every year — and which fund they go into. Unplanned extras can also be added from any month in the Calendar.

    Regular expenses

    Costs that repeat every month. Leave “From” and “Until” empty if they never change. One-off spends are added on their month’s page in the Calendar.

    + Add a one-off (pick a month)

    Loans & EMIs

    Enter the EMI, or the loan amount and interest rate to calculate it. When a loan ends, choose where that money goes.

    Savings goals & funds

    Only a name is required. Set a monthly amount to save automatically, or leave it empty and add money month by month from the Calendar. Leave the target empty for open-ended funds.

    Changes save automatically to the database on this computer.

    Click a row for the breakdown, or open the month to edit it.
